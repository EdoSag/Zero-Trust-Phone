Zero-Trust Phone replaces your phone app. It shows names from your encrypted Zero-Trust Contacts, silences spam and unknown callers on the phone itself, and keeps your call history private.

WHAT IT DOES
• Dialpad, recents, favourites and speed dial (long-press 2 to 9).
• Caller names from Zero-Trust Contacts, without giving other apps your address book.
• Spam and unknown-caller protection that runs on the phone, with no lookup service.
• Per-contact ringtones and "always send to voicemail".
• Decline with a text message, with your own quick replies.
• Missed-call notification with Call back, Message and Add to contacts.
• Recents with search, filters and a details page per person, plus call stats.
• Private call log: chosen contacts never stay in Android's shared call log.
• Encrypted call-history backup, so your history moves to a new phone.
• Paste any text and the dialer finds the number; dial with pauses for phone menus.
• Country and local time hint for international numbers, hide caller ID per call.
• Call back reminders that become a Zero-Trust Tasks task.
• Voicemail, Bluetooth car kits, Android Auto, RTT and carrier video calls.

WHY ZERO-TRUST
• End-to-end encrypted: everything is encrypted on your phone before it syncs. We can't read it, and neither can anyone who breaks into the server.
• One private account for every Zero-Trust app, unlocked with your fingerprint or face.
• Works offline and syncs across your devices when you're back online.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data sold. Ever.
• Open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check what the app does.
• English and Hebrew, with full right-to-left support.

Part of the Zero-Trust suite: Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Phone, Fitness, Meds, Time and Browser. One account opens them all.
